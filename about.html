ABOUT ME

Public service rooted in Lower Stoke

I have represented Lower Stoke since winning a by-election in February 2016. I combine council service with a full-time career in IT — keeping me grounded in the pressures working families face and focused on practical solutions.

What guides me

Approachable

No barriers or gatekeeping. Residents should be able to speak to their councillor.

Accountable

I believe in honest answers, scrutiny and following through on commitments.

Accessible

I stay visible in the ward and communicate online and in person.

Beyond the Council

I am a Labour & Co-operative councillor, a long-standing Co-operative Party regional secretary and an experienced workplace union branch secretary. I am active in campaigning, policy discussion and digital communication.

Languages

Fluent in English, Punjabi and Hindi, with conversational Urdu and other North Indian languages.

Whoever you are, whatever your faith or background, and however you voted, I will serve you equally.
